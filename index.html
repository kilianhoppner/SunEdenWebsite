Sun Eden is a haven for alternative modes of living. Founded by a number of dedicated environmentalists in 1995, it is now running its third decade of ever growing existence. Its vision is to grow into a sustainable, financially viable facility of local and international appeal, placing Sun Eden among the world’s leading sustainable resorts. People from all walks of life are welcome to experience the exhilarating freedom of being themselves, in nature, without judgement or prejudice. New residents quickly adapt to the rhythms of communal living and often choose to extend their stay. Annually upgraded facilities are provided.

As modern society continues to expand, Sun Eden strives to be the friendliest place. You will experience the friendly atmosphere while sitting around the campfire. Firewood and logs are supplied by Sun Eden. You bring your own meat and marshmallows. The following activities are available for residents: bird watching, gardening, beach ball, fishing, river floating, campfire and hiking. Participation is encouraged. Bask in the sun along the riverside or relax in the shade of our Bushveld trees. The day-to-day pressures of modern society are easily forgotten in this setting of natural beauty and tranquillity. Sun Eden is home to more than 100 species of birds, 4 types of buck, chickens, the large Banded Mongoose family, giraffes, grazing cattle and Bush Babies. Wildlife conservation is of paramount importance. Residents are reminded to respect quiet hours after sunset.
